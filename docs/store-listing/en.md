## Product name

Foldy File Manager

## Description

A fast, smart file manager. Even offline. Even better when you connect your AI.

Foldy is a free, open-source file manager that makes finding files, freeing up space and tidying your Desktop easy, in a window that feels just like File Explorer. Right-click menus, drag and drop and shortcuts work the same way, so you can start right away without a manual.

■ The four things people do most
• Find anything: press Ctrl+Space from anywhere and a big search box appears. Files, apps, Windows settings and math, all in one box. Type the way you talk, like "clean up my downloads", and Foldy finds the right tool.
• Free up space: see what fills your disk as big, simple boxes. Foldy only suggests things that are safe to delete, like temp files and caches, and deleting always goes to the Recycle Bin.
• Tidy your Desktop, Downloads and folders: Foldy first tells you what it found and what each button will do. Nothing is thrown away on its own, and one click on Undo puts everything back.
• Create folders from a template: pick a method like PARA, project management (PMBOK), research or photo archive, and the folders are made in one go.

■ Everyday work, made easier
• Two panes side by side: copy with drag and drop or one press of F5. Color tabs, and your folders are still open next time.
• Look without opening: F3 previews photos, documents, PDFs and code. Notes (.md) are shown nicely formatted.
• Slow drives don't freeze it: network drives and USB sticks show their list first.
• Undo mistakes: files overwritten while copying are kept aside for 7 days.
• Locked notes and vaults: password-protected notes, and vaults that lock several files together.
• Copying is fun: kids play basketball while your files copy. You can turn it off in Settings.
• For developers: see Git status the moment you open a folder, and open SFTP and SSH servers in a tab.

■ Even better when you connect your AI
Connect Foldy to an AI app like Claude, ask "find last year's quote", and the AI uses Foldy to find files on your PC. It is off by default, read-only when on, and only works inside this computer.

■ Use it with peace of mind
• Every feature works offline. No ads, no analytics, no tracking.
• Crash reports are off by default, and Foldy asks before sending each one.
• Choose a larger text size; dark and light mode follow Windows.
• Available in 한국어, English, Español, 日本語 and 简体中文.
• GPL-3.0 open source: https://github.com/chadolkr/Foldy

Good to know: the Store edition does not include the service that indexes whole drives ahead of time. Search looks through the folder you have open right now. Microsoft Store keeps Foldy up to date.

## What's new in this version

Improvements since 1.3.0:
• Tidying big folders: plans are made quickly even for hundreds of thousands of files, and you can continue later. See where identical files are and pick the one to keep.
• Find anything: shows how many results there are, and keeps loading as you scroll.
• Find folders safe to delete: shows progress, can be stopped, and finds more kinds.
• Six new folder templates.
• Slowly click a selected name once more to rename it.
• Simpler wording throughout.
